# NEXUS on Sony BRAVIA

This is a physical-TV demonstration of the NEXUS Android TV launcher.

- Device: Sony BRAVIA 4K GB, Android TV 9
- Feature: full-screen hero with clock, date and TV shortcuts
- Navigation: Sony remote D-pad focus transition into the app grid
- Source: [NEXUS Android project](nexus-launcher/README.md)
- Screenshot: [Home hero](docs/nexus/nexus-hero.png)
- Screenshot: [Focused application grid](docs/nexus/nexus-grid-focus.png)

The NEXUS source manifest does not request INTERNET permission.
Launching separate installed apps may still use the network.
